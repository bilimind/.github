# BiliMind

A study companion. Tell it what you're learning and by when, and it builds the course, plans each day, and checks that you actually learned it.

- **[bilimind.com](https://bilimind.com):** the app, free to start.
- **[Desktop](https://github.com/bilimind/desktop):** our open-source app. It turns your own slides, notes and past papers into a course, with every answer checked against your files, on your own AI key.

*Bilim* is the Uzbek word for knowledge.
