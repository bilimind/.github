<h1 align="center">BiliMind</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Your study companion. Learn anything. Actually finish it.</b></p>

Tell it what you're learning: your slides and deadlines, a Moodle course, or just a goal like "IELTS 7.0 by December". BiliMind lays the course out as a path, writes the lessons, plans your week, and checks a few days later that it stuck.

- **[bilimind.com](https://bilimind.com):** the app, free to start.
- **[bilimind.com/schools](https://bilimind.com/schools):** for learning centres and schools: groups, timetable, attendance, assignments, parents and fees, while every student studies in their own BiliMind.
- **[Desktop](https://github.com/bilimind/desktop):** the open-source companion that runs on your own computer with your own AI key (AGPL-3.0).

*Bilim* is the Uzbek word for knowledge. Bili is the little one who keeps you company.
